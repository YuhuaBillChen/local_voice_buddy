01 — 04

01Voice Mode · 直接对话

Ask a question out loud, hear the answer, then follow up.

开口提问，听它回答，再接着追问。

OPEN WEBUI v0.12.3 · LIVE CAPTURE
“Why does it rain?” Hear the complete explanation, then ask what to bring outside. Speech starts from early text chunks while the rest of the reply is generated.“为什么天空会下雨？” 听完完整解释，再追问出门要带什么。前面的文字先合成播放，后面的回答继续生成。

02Voice Input · 语音输入

Speak, review the text, edit it, then send.

说完先看文字，还能修改，再发送。

Dictate a message about arriving ten minutes late, then add a tone instruction before sending it.口述“告诉朋友我会晚到十分钟”，再加上“语气自然一点”发送。

03Read Aloud · 回复朗读

One click turns a written reply into speech.

点一下扬声器，听它读出文字回复。

The actual chat reply is synthesized by CosyVoice3 and played in the browser.普通聊天里的实际回复，由 CosyVoice3 合成后在浏览器播放。

04Visual Answers · 可视化回答

Give it some numbers and ask for a chart; it is drawn right in the chat.

给它几个数字、让它画个图，图表直接画在聊天里。

Monthly spending as a pie chart, about 40 seconds from asking to the finished drawing. The 27B draws it with the Open Intelligent UI components, all on the PC. Typed chats only: in a voice call answers stay spoken.把每月开销画成饼图，从提问到画完四十秒左右。27B 用 Open Intelligent UI 组件画出来，全部在本机运行。只在打字聊天里使用，语音通话里回答仍然是说出来的。

A smoother conversation.

The small details that matter when you use it for more than one turn.

从开口到听见回答，再到下一句，都要接得上。

LISTEN

Keep the whole question.

首音和尾音都保住。

A short microphone buffer keeps the beginning of your speech. Recognition can start during your final pause.

短音频缓冲保住开头；说完后的停顿里，识别就可以提前开始。

RESPOND

Speak sentence by sentence.

逐句回答，不等长文写完。

Chinese and English replies reach speech synthesis in short chunks. Playback starts when the first chunk is ready; it does not need the full reply.

中文和英文都分块送去合成，第一块准备好就开始播放；首声仍需要文字分块和合成的时间。

CONTINUE

Finish a thought, then follow up.

听完一句，再接着聊。

Listen to the complete reply and ask your next question. Leaving a call also leaves regular read-aloud ready to use.

让它完整说完，再问下一句。退出通话后，普通聊天的朗读仍然可用。

One PC. Three jobs.

一台主机，听、想、说。

Use the GPU for the big model and the NPU for speech acceleration.

大模型用核显，语音用 NPU 加速。

Whisper
LISTEN · XDNA NPU + CPU

Recognizes your speech. The encoder runs on the NPU and the decoder on the CPU.

把语音变成文字。编码器跑在 NPU，解码器跑在 CPU。

Qwen3.8 27B
THINK · RADEON iGPU

A 27-billion-parameter open model generates the reply locally.

270 亿参数的开源模型，在本机生成回答。

CosyVoice3
SPEAK · XDNA NPU + CPU

Synthesizes the answer. Its flow decoder uses the NPU, with other stages on the CPU. You can add a voice from a reference recording.

把回答说出来。Flow 解码器使用 NPU，其余阶段使用 CPU；也能用参考录音添加音色。

Everything runs on your PC. The installer downloads the software and models once; after that your voice and your chats stay on your own machine, even when you talk to it from your phone.

全部在你自己的电脑上运行。安装时下载一次软件和模型，之后你的声音和聊天记录都留在自己的机器上，用手机跟它说话也一样。

Phones and response time手机使用与响应时间

Browsers only allow the microphone on https pages, so talking from a phone needs one extra step; the README shows how. On a 32 GB Ryzen 7 8845HS the reply starts speaking about 8 to 13 seconds after you stop talking (about 20 for the very first message after a restart).

浏览器只在 https 页面上允许用麦克风，所以用手机说话要多做一步，README 里有说明。在 32 GB 的锐龙 7 8845HS 上，说完话后大约 8 到 13 秒开始听到回答（重启后的第一句约 20 秒）。
